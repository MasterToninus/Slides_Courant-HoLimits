	\textbf{Abstract:}
	\\
In symplectic geometry, classical observables form a Poisson algebra. For
higher-degree analogues of symplectic structures, this algebraic structure is
replaced by an $L_\infty$-algebra, whose higher brackets encode the geometry of
observables and their symmetries. Higher Courant algebroids provide a natural
ambient framework for studying these structures and the morphisms relating
them.

In \cite{Zambon2012}, Marco Zambon constructs an $L_\infty$-algebra associated
with any standard higher Courant algebroid and asks whether canonical
$L_\infty$-morphisms from twisted to untwisted higher Courant algebras exist in
arbitrary degree. We answer this question affirmatively in \cite{Fiorenza2026}.
The key ingredient is the realization of the relevant $L_\infty$-algebras as
homotopy fibers of natural morphisms between differential graded Lie algebras.
The same framework accommodates the observable morphism of \cite{Miti2024}.

Time permitting, I will discuss ongoing work with Aditya Basu and Domenico
Fiorenza extending this perspective from graphs of closed differential forms
to general higher Dirac structures.
